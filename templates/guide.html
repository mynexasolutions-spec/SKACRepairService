Mastering Your Cooling

Proper maintenance can extend AC life by years and lower summer electricity bills by up to 20%.

Maintenance

The 15-Day Filter Rule

Summer dust in Chandigarh & Mohali can clog indoor AC filters quickly. Rinsing filters every 15 days prevents compressor strain and boosts airflow immediately.

Energy Saving

The Perfect 24°C Balance

Setting your AC to 18°C doesn't cool faster; it just keeps the compressor running longer. 24°C is the ideal human comfort balance and saves maximum energy.

Insulate Copper Pipes Properly

Damaged foam insulation on outdoor copper pipes causes cooling loss before refrigerant even enters your room. Ensure copper lines stay fully insulated.

Fabrics & Machine Health

Avoid common mistakes that lead to drum damage and expensive motor repairs.

Longevity

Monthly Drum Descale

Hard water across Tricity sectors can cause white scale buildup inside the drum. Run a monthly tub-clean cycle with descaler to protect the drum and heater.

Operation

Don't Overload the Drum

Stuffing the drum puts heavy pressure on bearings, shockers, and motor belts. Always leave a hand's gap at the top for smooth spinning.

Noise inspection →
Smart Use

Right Detergent, Right Amount

Excess detergent creates foam overflow and harms the digital PCB. Always match Front-Load or Top-Load detergent to your machine type.

The Paper Test for Gaskets

Close a sheet of paper in the fridge door. If it slips out effortlessly, your door seal is leaking cool air and overworking the compressor.

Storage

Don't Block Air Vents

Cold air needs open paths to circulate from freezer to fridge compartment. Blocking vents causes uneven cooling and ice buildup.
