3 90-Day Service Warranty & Guarantee

Comprehensive 90-Day Workmanship Warranty

All repairs, part replacements, and gas charging jobs performed by SK AC Repair Services carry a 90-day service warranty against recurring defects.

  • If the exact same issue recurs within 90 days from the service date, our technician will inspect and rectify it with zero labor charge.
  • Replaced components carry the standard manufacturer / supplier warranty.
  • Warranty Exceptions: The warranty is void if:
    • The appliance has been opened, repaired, or tampered with by any third party or non-authorized person after our service.

4 Copper Pipe Fitting & Installation Policy

  • We use 100% pure heavy-gauge copper pipes with nitrile rubber thermal insulation and fire-retardant tape.
  • For concealed (underground / inside wall) piping in new constructions, nitrogen pressure testing is performed to ensure 100% leak-proof joints before plastering.
  • Civil wall-chasing (grooving) and pipe securing are done with utmost care; however, final wall plastering, putty, and paint finishing remain the property owner's or builder's responsibility unless explicitly contracted.
  • Standard installation packages include mounting of indoor and outdoor units with standard accessories. Any civil core cutting on reinforced concrete beams or specialized scaffolding for high-rise buildings will be pre-agreed with the client.
