Overview, Scope & Privacy Commitment

How we treat your personal data

Welcome to SK AC Repair Services ("we", "our", "us", or "skacrepairservice.in"). We provide professional doorstep air conditioning repair, installation, gas charging, copper pipe fitting, washing machine repair, refrigerator repair, geyser maintenance, and old AC buying across Chandigarh, Mohali, Kharar, Zirakpur, and Panchkula.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, store, and safeguard your personal details when you browse our website, call our customer care numbers, message us on WhatsApp, or schedule a doorstep service appointment. We treat your personal data with utmost confidentiality and never sell or rent your information to third-party marketing companies.

Our Privacy Commitment: We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver prompt, reliable doorstep repairs and transparent customer service. Your data is never sold, traded, or leased.

Security & Data Retention

Protecting customer records

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

We retain your contact and service history only for as long as necessary to fulfill the warranty, maintain maintenance logs (such as AC gas charging or copper piping warranty), or comply with tax and legal requirements. When records are no longer required, they are securely purged or anonymized.
